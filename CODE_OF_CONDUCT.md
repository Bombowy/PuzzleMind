# Code of Conduct

## Our commitment

LogicForge contributors, maintainers, and participants commit to making project
spaces welcoming, safe, and respectful for everyone, regardless of background,
identity, experience level, or personal characteristics.

## Expected behavior

- Communicate with empathy, patience, and technical clarity.
- Give and accept constructive feedback focused on the work.
- Respect differing viewpoints and levels of experience.
- Credit ideas and contributions accurately.
- Protect private information and report security concerns responsibly.

## Unacceptable behavior

Harassment, discrimination, threats, sexualized attention, personal attacks,
doxxing, sustained disruption, and other conduct that a reasonable person would
consider hostile or unsafe are not tolerated in project spaces.

## Enforcement

Maintainers may edit or remove contributions and may temporarily or permanently
restrict participation when behavior violates this policy. Enforcement decisions
should be fair, confidential where practical, and proportionate to the incident.

Report conduct concerns privately to the repository owner through their GitHub
profile. Include relevant context and evidence. Maintainers will protect reporter
privacy as far as reasonably possible and will not tolerate retaliation.

This policy applies in repositories, issue trackers, review threads, community
channels, and public spaces where someone represents LogicForge.
